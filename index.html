About

I am currently a PhD student in applied mathematics at the Institut de Mathématiques de Bordeaux (IMB) of Université de Bordeaux. My thesis, entitled "Stochastic algorithms in high dimensions with applications in statistical learning", is supervised by Bernard Bercu and Luis Fredes.

My research revolves around the convergence analysis of stochastic algorithms for optimization as well as their asymptotic properties via notably martingale theory.

Stochastic algorithms Stochastic optimization Martingale theory Statistical learning
